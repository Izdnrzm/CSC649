# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
The stakeholders that will be affected by the problem are maintenance technicians, system operators, engineers, and facility managers. For safe and efficient operation of equipment these users require continuous access to monitoring information. When monitoring is limited to fixed control stations, users may experience delays in accessing critical data, resulting in slower responses to abnormal conditions and potential operational inefficiencies.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Current monitoring systems often rely on fixed control panels or desktop computers located in a control room. As a result, operators and maintenance personnel cannot easily access real time monitoring data while moving around the facility. When abnormal conditions occur, users may not receive immediate updates, leading to delayed responses and reduced operational efficiency. This lack of mobility and accessibility makes monitoring less effective, especially in environments where continuous observation is required.

## Why does it matter?
This problem matters because timely access to monitoring data is critical for ensuring system reliability, operational efficiency, and safety. When operators and maintenance personnel cannot monitor equipment in real time while moving around a facility, abnormal conditions may go unnoticed or be detected too late. This can lead to equipment damage, operational downtime, increased maintenance costs, and potential safety risks. By providing a portable monitoring interface through the M5Stack Cardputer, users can respond more quickly to issues, improving both productivity and system performance.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Industrial and environmental monitoring systems often require operators to access monitoring data through fixed computers or dedicated control panels. This limits mobility and makes real time monitoring difficult when operators are away from the control center. This limitation can result in delayed detection of abnormal conditions, slower response to system faults, and reduced operational efficiency.
